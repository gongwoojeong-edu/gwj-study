The 2026 Footnote Dance Program is a modern dance program created for youth aged 13-18. It runs from Wednesday, October 21st to Friday, October 23rd, from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. each day, at Footnote Studio, 70 Elm Street. It costs $50 per workshop, or $120 for all three workshops. On Day 1, the Dance Lesson helps you build creative confidence through guided movement exercises. On Day 2, Performance Practice refines your performance skills and teaches you to connect with your audience. On Day 3, Open Studio invites friends and family to see what participants have created. A free shuttle bus service will be provided for all participants and visitors. Pre-registration is required.
