The graph above shows the willingness to pay for access to sports content across five different age groups, ranging from 16-24 to 55-64. The proportion of respondents who are Willing to pay is highest in the 16-24 age group, and it is lowest in the 55-64 age group. The percentage of those who are Willing to pay in the 25-34 age group is higher than that of those who are Willing to pay in the 35-44 age group. The 45-54 age group has the same percentage of respondents who are Very unwilling to pay and Unwilling to pay. In the 55-64 age group, the combined percentage of those who are Very willing and Willing to pay is above 25 percent. Throughout the five age groups, the percentage of those who are Very unwilling to pay increases as the age range goes up.
