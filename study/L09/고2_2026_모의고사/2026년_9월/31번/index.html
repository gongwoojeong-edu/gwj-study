Human geography is becoming unclear. As people find themselves regularly on the move, we are experiencing a phase shift like when matter transitions from solid to liquid to gas: molecules heat up and loosen from one another, vibrating more rapidly. One might even say that humans are becoming like particles in quantum physics, their velocity and location always in flux. It would be nice to return to some form of stability, but that's not how things work in a quantum world. Instead, the complexity of today's world makes it increasingly difficult to permanently settle anywhere. Highly paid digital nomads and billionaires with multiple passports as well as migratory workers are all part of the diverse and growing global demographic of quantum people.

사람들이 끊임없이 이동하면서 인간 지리학은 불명확해지고 있다. 오늘날의 복잡한 세계에서 인간은 양자 물리학의 입자처럼 속도와 위치가 항상 유동적인 상태로 변화하고 있으며, 영구적으로 정착하기가 점점 더 어려워지고 있다.
